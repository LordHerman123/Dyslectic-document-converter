How well documents come out

Automatic scores for every document in the test set: is all the text there, in the right order, without page clutter, with its headings, and made of real words. Higher is better; 100% is perfect.

Score per kind of document

The overall score is the average of the scores that apply to a document. The AI-assisted column fills in when the benchmark is run with an AI key.

Text recognition (OCR)

Each text PDF was also scanned (its first three pages as a slightly skewed 200 dpi image, as from a copier) and converted with text recognition only. The gap between the two dots is what reading a scan costs.

Web pages

Each page has a list of its article's paragraphs that must be kept and of the clutter that must go (menus, cookie notices, share buttons, related links, footers). Test pages are modelled on common site layouts; the FiveThirtyEight page is a real saved page.

What the scores mean

Content kept
Words of the original found in the conversion. Figures and formulas shown as pictures of the original, and running headers and footers, are not counted as lost.
Nothing added
Words of the conversion found in the original. Garbled or invented words lower it.
Sentences intact
Runs of five words of the original that are still together: the reading order is right, columns are not mixed, words are not split.
Clutter removed
Running headers, footers and page numbers of the original that did not end up in the text.
Headings found
The PDF's own bookmarks found as headings (only PDFs with bookmarks, and web pages).
Real words
Share of words that are dictionary words, compared with the original's own share (names and jargon count the same on both sides). For scans without a known text, the plain share.
Article kept
Web pages: the article's paragraphs found in the conversion.

Keep in mind

  • The scores are measured by the computer, not by a reader. They catch lost, mixed-up and garbled text reliably; they do not judge how pleasant a page is to read.
  • Real scans (old books, photocopies) have no known text, so only clutter and real words are measured for them.
  • Every run uses the same settings (the Standard preset, citations left in their sentences), so runs can be compared.

Running it again

The documents stay on your computer (most are published papers). The key for the AI-assisted run is read from its environment variable and never saved.

python tools/benchmark.py run --docs FOLDER
python tools/benchmark.py run --docs FOLDER --ai mistral      # also AI-assisted (MISTRAL_API_KEY)
python tools/benchmark.py compare benchmarks/results/1.15.0-local.json benchmarks/results/NEW.json
python tools/benchmark_report.py benchmarks/results/NEW.json benchmarks/results/1.15.0-local.json
