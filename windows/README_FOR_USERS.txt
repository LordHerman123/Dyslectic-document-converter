Dyslexia Converter
==================

Converts PDFs (articles, book chapters, scans) into a cleaner, more readable
layout: larger type, more spacing, one calm column, easy to print on A4.
The author's words are never changed, and your original PDF is never modified.

Getting started
---------------
1. Double-click DyslexiaConverter.exe.
   (Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" the first time, because the
   program is new and not code-signed: click "More info", then "Run anyway".)
2. Click "Open PDF" and choose a file.
3. Adjust the settings on the left; the preview on the right updates.
4. Click one of the Export buttons (PDF, Printable PDF, Word, ...) to save.

Scanned books and photocopies are recognised automatically. Text recognition
(Tesseract) is included, so nothing else needs to be installed.

Everything happens on your computer. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you
switch on the optional AI features and enter your own API key.

Keep all files in this folder together: the program needs the files next to
DyslexiaConverter.exe (including the "tesseract" folder).

Licences of the included fonts and programs are in the LICENSES folder.
